EcoRewards SA 🌿
**A Smart Recycling Rewards System for eThekwini Municipality Residents**

Live Demo: https://ecorewards-sa-cmeeangqfzegcfcg.centralindia-01.azurewebsites.net

📋 Problem Statement

Recycling participation in South African municipalities remains low because residents have no tangible incentive to sort and drop off recyclable materials, and municipalities have no reliable data on what is actually being recycled at household level.

EcoRewards SA gives residents a way to log their recycling activity and earn reward points redeemable against municipal services or partner vouchers. It gives collection officers a verification tool so only genuine recycling activity earns points. And it gives administrators reporting on community-wide recycling volumes and environmental impact so the municipality can demonstrate progress against sustainability targets.

 ✨ Features

### Resident
- Register and log in with role-based access control
- Log recycling submissions by material type, weight, and drop-off point
- Track submission status — Pending, Verified, or Rejected
- View real-time points balance and full points transaction history
- View personal environmental impact report (CO₂ saved with real-world equivalents)
- Browse the community leaderboard — monthly and all-time rankings
- Browse and register for community collection events
- Redeem points for rewards including municipal bill discounts, vouchers, and airtime
- Receive in-app notifications on submission outcomes
- Earn achievement badges for recycling milestones

### Collection Officer
- View all pending submissions at assigned drop-off points
- Verify or reject submissions with a mandatory rejection reason
- Automatic point calculation and resident notification on verification
- View complete submission history with status filters

### Administrator
- Live dashboard with Chart.js bar and line charts showing recycling trends
- Manage material types, drop-off points, and collection officers
- Schedule and manage community collection events
- Generate monthly community recycling reports
- Export reports to PDF and Excel
- Manage rewards catalogue and view redemption requests
